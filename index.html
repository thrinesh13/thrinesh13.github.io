Medicare Claims Payment Integrity

Reviewing every claim by hand was modeled at $425K, and improper payments still got through. Which claims can billing rules decide safely?

ApproachCleaned 10 raw claims extracts into business-ready tables in Python and PostgreSQL, then built 10 SQL prepayment edits from Medicare Part B billing rules and tested them against the payer's decisions.

RecommendationAuto-deny on the 6 most reliable edits, send 4 to review, and start provider education with the top 30 providers, who drive 63% of flagged claims. Tracked in a 4-page Power BI report.

Hospital operations · Data pipeline · Variance analysis

Surgical Scheduling Variance Analysis

Surgeries are booked on duration estimates that are rarely checked. How often do they miss, and what does it cost?

ApproachCleaned 65.7K raw operating room records into a business-ready 48.1K-case cohort in Microsoft Fabric, then benchmarked every surgery against its procedure's median duration in Power BI.

RecommendationReview the 133 procedures driving over half the variation first, and set booking times per procedure to reduce both overruns and the 2,167 idle room hours a year from early finishes.
