Medicare Claims Payment Integrity

Manually reviewing every claim was modeled at $425K, and improper payments still got through. I tested which claims rules can safely decide before payment.

ApproachCleaned 10 raw claims extracts in Python and built staging, core, and mart layers in PostgreSQL. Translated Medicare Part B billing rules into 10 SQL prepayment edits and validated them against payer decisions.

OutcomeRules can route 96% of claims at 93.3% edit accuracy, tracked in a 4-page Power BI report with drill-through KPIs and what-if cost scenarios. Human review stays for medical necessity and missing documentation, where rules fall short.

Hospital operations · Data pipeline · Variance analysis

Surgical Scheduling Variance Analysis

Operating rooms are among a hospital's most expensive resources, yet surgeries are booked on duration estimates that are rarely checked. I measured how often they miss and what it costs.

ApproachBuilt a Microsoft Fabric medallion pipeline in PySpark on 1.88M records, cleaning 65.7K operating room records into a 48.1K-case cohort. Benchmarked each procedure against its median in a Power BI star-schema model.

OutcomeThe variance is concentrated in a small set of procedures, so correcting their duration estimates first targets the largest share of cost exposure.

Microsoft FabricPySparkPower BIDAX

Stock market trend analysis · Statistical testing

Statistical Time-Series Analysis

Apple's stock more than doubled in five years, through a 2022 selloff and a 2023 recovery. I tested four common beliefs about how a stock like this behaves.

ApproachCleaned 1,254 trading days in pandas and ran a statistical time-series analysis using trend, correlation, distribution, and hypothesis testing.

OutcomeGrowth did not bring more risk, volume did not predict price, and calendar patterns did not hold year to year. The real risk is in sudden big swings, which happen several times a year.
