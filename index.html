01 · HEALTHCARE OPERATIONS · BI ANALYTICS

OR Scheduling Reliability & Cost Exposure

Built a Microsoft Fabric pipeline and Power BI model to identify procedures with unpredictable durations and prioritize operational review.

BUSINESS QUESTION

Which procedures vary most from their historical duration benchmark, and where should an operations team investigate first?

  • Staged approximately 1.9M source records; cleaned patient information into 48,118 qualifying surgical cases.
  • Built a star-schema analytical model and Power BI semantic model with reusable DAX measures.
  • Analyzed 418 procedures and quantified a 54.1-minute mean absolute difference from historical procedure medians.
  • Estimated $15.8M–$27.1M in annual gross time-cost exposure using $35/min and $60/min planning scenarios.

Historical benchmarks, rather than booked time slots. Cost exposure is a planning scenario, not measured loss or realized savings.
